# Altostrat Employee Policy Handbook: Leave & Paid Time Off (July 2026)

## Section 1: Sick Leave & Hospitalization
- **Outpatient Sick Leave Allowance**: Eligible employees and interns receive up to **14 days of paid outpatient sick leave** per calendar year, compensated at 100% of base salary.
- **Hospitalization Leave Allowance**: Employees receive an additional **46 work days of paid hospitalization leave** per year for inpatient stays, day surgeries, quarantine orders, or serious medical conditions.
- **Notification Requirements**: Must notify manager at least **1 hour before normal start time**.
- **Medical Certificate (MC) Submission**: For sick leave exceeding **2 work days**, a valid MC from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted via WorkWeek within **48 hours**.

## Section 2: Paid Vacation Leave
- **Accrual Tier Matrix**:
  - 1 to 6 years of service: **20 days per year**.
  - 7 to 10 years of service: **21 days per year**.
  - 11+ years of service: **22 days per year**.
- **Proration**: First-year employees receive prorated vacation based on start date.
- **Booking and Scheduling Rules**:
  - Vacation can be taken in half-day or full-day increments.
  - Planned vacation dates must be submitted and approved at least **15 days in advance**.
  - Changes or cancellations must be processed at least **15 days before leave starts**.
- **Carryover & Pay-out Limitations**: Unused vacation days carry over for exactly one additional year (must be used by December 31 of following year or forfeited). Unused vacation is not paid out except upon country-to-country transfer or termination.
- **Floating Holidays**: If a recognized public holiday falls on a weekend, employees receive a floating holiday to be used like vacation before year-end.

## Section 3: Childcare & Family-Building Leave
- **Childcare Leave Allowance**:
  - Children under 7 years old: **6 days of paid leave per year**.
  - Youngest child 7–12 years old: **2 days of paid leave per year**.
  - Children in both age groups: **6 days of paid leave per year**.
- **Maternity Leave**: **24 weeks of paid parental leave** (effective April 1, 2026). Can extend up to 26 weeks if spouse donates Shared Parental Leave (SPL). First 8 weeks (56 days) must be taken consecutively. Notice requirement is **4 weeks**.
- **Baby Bonding Leave (BBL)**: Up to **18 weeks (90 work days)** of paid leave per year for non-birthing parents, usable within 12 months of birth/placement. Includes monetary baby bonding benefit. Notice requirement is **4 weeks**.
- **Ramp-Back Time**: Up to **2 weeks of paid ramp-back time** immediately upon return from long leave (minimum 10 consecutive weeks of bonding/maternity leave), working at least 50% time for 100% salary.

## Section 4: Compassionate, Unpaid & Special Leaves
- **Bereavement Leave**: Up to **4 weeks (20 work days)** of paid leave per event for loss of a close loved one (includes pregnancy loss). Must be taken within 12 months. Pet loss is excluded.
- **Carer's Leave**: Up to **8 weeks of paid leave** per loved one per lifetime to care for seriously or terminally ill family members/dependents.
- **Unpaid Time Off**: Up to **30 calendar days** with manager approval.
- **Personal Leave**: Requests past 30 days reclassify as Personal Leave (up to **92 calendar days** with manager and director approval). Requires **30 days advance notice**.
- **Time Off in Lieu (TOIL)**: Granted at manager discretion for required work on public holidays or weekends. Must be used before logging additional vacation.
