# Altostrat Employee Policy Handbook: Hardware, Ethics & Conduct (July 2026)

## Section 1: Hardware Provisioning & Allowance
- **ITSM Equipment Ordering**: All hardware (monitors, security keys, laptops, accessories) must be ordered exclusively by opening a case in **ITSM (ServiceImmediately)** with manager approval.
- **Home Office Equipment Allowance**: Employees with approved 'Remote' or 'Hybrid' location status receive a **US $500 allowance** for home office equipment (monitors, ergonomics) via Facilities category ITSM ticket.

## Section 2: Ethics, Gifts & Commercial Boundaries
- **Anti-Bribery & Government Officials**: Zero tolerance for bribes or facilitation payments. Pre-approval via Risk, Compliance & Integrity (RCI) is required for any item given to U.S. government officials, or over US $100 for non-U.S. government officials.
- **Commercial Gifts & Entertainment (Non-Government)**:
  - Under US $100 per person: No pre-approval required.
  - US $100 to US $250: Manager written pre-approval.
  - US $250 to US $500: Director written pre-approval.
  - Over US $500: VP written pre-approval.
  - Frequency Limit: No more than twice in any 3-month period with the same partner. Cash, gift cards, gambling, or adult entertainment are strictly prohibited.

## Section 3: Workplace Conduct & Data Security
- **Supervisory Personal Relationships**: Romantic or physical relationships are strictly prohibited between employees where one individual supervises or exercises authority over the other. VP level and above must disclose all such relationships regardless of reporting line.
- **Remote Work Data Security**: Confidential projects must not be worked on in public settings (coffee shops, libraries). Must use privacy screens and headphones during meetings.
- **Community Guidelines**: Disrupting WorkWeek for political or non-work debates is prohibited. Zero tolerance for trolling, doxxing, or harassment.
