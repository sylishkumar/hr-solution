# Altostrat Employee Policy Handbook: Travel & Expense (T&E) Guidelines (July 2026)

## Section 1: Pre-Travel & Booking Timelines
- **Advance Booking Mandate**: All airfare and hotel bookings must be completed at least **3 weeks (21 days) in advance** through the TRIPS portal to secure reasonable rates.
- **Pre-Travel Checklist**: Discuss trip with manager, review travel caps, and log an ITSM Travel case prior to departure.

## Section 2: Expense Limits & Allowances
- **Daily Meal Limit**: Individual business meal reimbursement is capped at **US $120 per day**. Itemized receipts are required.
- **Group Meals**: Capped at **US $120 per employee per day**. Must list all attendees in Concur. Senior-most colleague present must pay.
- **VP Pre-Approval for High-Value Events**: Group meals or entertainment events costing **US $500 or greater per head** require written pre-event approval from VP attached in Concur.
- **Staying with Friends/Family**: Host gift allowance up to **US $50 per day** backed by receipts. Cash or gift card host gifts are strictly prohibited.

## Section 3: Corporate Cards & Submission Timelines
- **Company Card Mandate**: Mandatory for employees incurring >$10,000 per quarter in expenses or single transactions over $5,000.
- **Submission Window**: All travel expenses must be submitted through Concur within **30 days** of transaction or travel end date.
- **Aged Expense Approvals**:
  - Out-of-pocket claims older than **60 days** require Director approval.
  - Claims older than **90 days** require VP approval.
  - Claims older than **1 year** are non-reimbursable.
